<p>This year I went to my first DEF CON (see <a href="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/2024/06/24/defcon32-announcement/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">DEF CON 32 Announcement</a>).</p>

<p>I had an amazing time, met cool people, and learned a lot! I may be hooked. I kept thinking "Next time I will..." ... </p>

<h2>Travel</h2>

<p>Oof, travel there was rough! I thought I'd easily be able to sleep during my overnight layover, but no not at all! It was far too noisy. So this wasn't a great start. </p>

<figure><a href="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-10.png"><img src="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-10.png?w=1024" alt="" /></a><figcaption>Orlando airport sleeping</figcaption></figure>

<p>I flew with Frontier airlines. In the future, I think I will only consider them for short trips, like visiting my mother. I think for longer trips, where one wants ample baggage and amenities, other airlines are better. I'm just not sure that I saved much money, especially not enough to justify the layover. I enjoyed seeing the grand canyon and wind turbines from the airport window.</p>

<figure><a href="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-11.png"><img src="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-11.png?w=1024" alt="" /></a><figcaption>windmills</figcaption></figure>

<p>I did manage to read almost all of Plague of Fantasies by Zizek. I was surprisingly tickled and intrigued by the book, which posed a lot of interesting and horny thoughts about how we interface with reality. I found myself reflecting on it a lot. Hackers leaving cyberspace to meet "in person" to discuss cyberspace... This was very complementary to what was being explored in the book. Hacker itself is an identity that is in many ways fantastic... and that's very much because of the fantastic nature of cyberspace.</p>

<figure><a href="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-7.png"><img src="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-7.png?w=658" alt="" /></a><figcaption>Adidas 3D printed shoe</figcaption></figure>

<h2>Lodgings</h2>

<p>I stayed in the room block at the Rio hotel. The lodgings were humble... Not perfectly clean and lacking a coffee maker or microwave.  The sectional couch was cool. Overall, did the trick.</p>

<figure><a href="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-15.png"><img src="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-15.png?w=609" alt="" /></a><figcaption>hotel room sectional</figcaption></figure>

<p>The shuttle was very much an asset. Was definitely the best value that I was getting out of being at the Rio. I never faced any problems getting to and from the LVCC.</p>

<p>I enjoyed a dip in the pool and hot tubs as well. I usually think its more fun to do that sort of thing with friends, but the day was gorgeous and it felt nice to be in the water. So I actually kind of enjoyed it.</p>

<figure><a href="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-9.png"><img src="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-9.png?w=1024" alt="" /></a><figcaption>In a Rio hot tub</figcaption></figure>

<h2>The Badge</h2>

<p>Because I am a demo lab presenter, I was given a speaker badge, which is epic, I can't understate how honored I am and how precious this gadget is to me now. It was fairly straightforward to locate the area where demo labs folks pick up their badge, and I had a small chit chat with the organizers that helped me steel my nerves. I'm going to check if maybe I can be a demo labs goon next year. I would love to give back.</p>

<h2>Demo lab</h2>

<p>Happily, I had some attendees to my demo lab. Probably about 30 overall. I really hope I hear from those folks. After my slides, I would invite people to the front to experience the hardware, which I saw as the best advantage of being at an in person talk, and got about 8 folks to do so. I was able to give them a lot more details.</p>

<p>I updated the <a href="https://forum.defcon.org/node/249627">demo labs forums</a> with my files and happily my demo lab is now part of the <a href="https://infocondb.org/con/def-con/def-con-32/open-hardware-design-for-buskill-cord">infodb</a>. It feels really good to have been a part of this!</p>

<figure><a href="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-4.png"><img src="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-4.png?w=658" alt="" /></a><figcaption>My listing in the program</figcaption></figure>

<p>I particularly enjoyed meeting Joe Fitz of securinghardware.com. We had an interesting conversation with the folks that came to the front. His comments have inspired me to go ahead and perhaps pursue one PCB version of the BusKill project... just for kicks. He gave me a Tigard board which has induced me on a rewarding learning path.</p>

<p>There were a lot of neat stickers on every table in the LVCC. Now both my laptops and water bottle look epic. Definitely the soldering girl sticker is next level. But I love all my stickers.</p>

<h2>Villages</h2>

<figure><a href="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image.png"><img src="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image.png?w=1024" alt="" /></a><figcaption>Forgot where this was</figcaption></figure>

<p>I didn't manage to actually participate in any village activities, but I walked around and observed. I was really happy to see a large group of people learning OpenSCAD. If I come back, I will bring some of my spare parts to the Soldering Village because I have a lot of fun stuff they could probably use that I have no use for...... back from over  a decade ago when I didn't know how to do hardware and thought I could just buy a bunch of random parts and make stuff with them lol...</p>

<p>I asked one of the Soldering Village leads if he had any protips about soldering to USBs. He told me about using artist's kneaded erasers for stabilizing parts and gave me a cool 3D printed solder dispenser.</p>

<p>I picked up the Scavenger Hunt but I forgot to read it during the con. I read it after and realized that it would have been beneficial to read it when I got it, but nevertheless I still managed to mark off a few items without trying.</p>

<h2>Meetups</h2>

<p>On Thursday, I went a little too hard on the dragon staff. I was mainly trying to get some practice in, but I didn't know there was gonna be a late industrial set and I pushed myself through it even though I was already exhausted. I bruised up quite a lot, too.</p>

<p>I was fashionably late to the ATL meetup, but that was a pretty nice event. They had some multiplayer games, beverages, and an inviting atmosphere. I was able to chat to a few cool folks.</p>

<p>Friday night... I meant to go to gothcon but I ate a bunch of vegan dim sum and crashed instead. I will note here that I survived con almost entirely on "walking tamales".  This walking tamales diet led to me actually decreasing an entire pant size, which is a bit insane.</p>

<p>Saturday, I spent some time making friendship bracelets at the Women in Security and Privacy area. I had a nice chat with the folks there. I made two so that I would have one for Jack Rhysider and one for someone else if the occasion arose. This was fortunate, because I actually broke one of my bracelets that I made, so because I made two, it wasn't a total disaster... </p>

<figure><a href="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-14.png"><img src="https://crashxblossom.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-14.png?w=493" alt="" /></a><figcaption>friendship bracelet</figcaption></figure>

<p>Jack Rhysider's party was pretty awesome. I had to wait in line for 2 hours for it, but worth it. Plus, I had fun talking to the people in line. Once we got to the party, the music and atmosphere was spot on. Cool interactive displays, darknet diaries swag, and Jack Rhysider was walking around with this cool light up staff thing to mark where he was in the room.  At one point, a person became very interested in seeing what I could do with the dragon staff, so despite my protests, she encouraged all the dancers to give me a space to flow up near the stage. Haha, I was definitely pretty tired and a bit anxious to spin in such close proximity to people (even with her safetying), but I tried to rise to the occasion and I hope I managed to put on a good enough show for them to justify the space we took :).</p>

<p>Wasn't really able to chat with Rhysider but did manage to exchange bracelets. Well basically, I was sort of in queue to say hi to him, when the door suddenly started opening. I had just reached him and I was like <em>oh shit maybe the party is ending and he needs to be somewhere</em>. And in a semi-panic I was like "like everyone here, big fan of the podcast, you're awesome, I made this bracelet for you and uh can I get a pic with our shoes?" and then I just like, took the picture lol. Uh, it was probably slightly cringe, I don't know. I hope that he still has the bracelet because I didn't know the proper tying technique when I made it... In any case, he's pretty active in the Discord with all 25000 of us fans so... senpai can notice me any time he wants lol. </p>

<h2>After the con</h2>

<p>I did unfortunately get the latest version of the COVID-19 virus. It knocked me out bad for a week. When I was awake, I did DEF CON things... Like network online with people I met, or read about my new gadgets or things I heard, or watch other people's recaps.</p>

<p>I feel inspired to not only deepen my pursuit of hardware, programming, and opsec, but also to make quality content during the journey. I want to take my  documentation and story telling skills to the next level and just put a bit more into the production value sometimes. I know that this is something that will take time and energy, but I see a trend of really good stuff, and I know how I feel when on the receiving end, so, I want to be a part of this radical knowledge sharing.</p>

<h2>My homework</h2>

<p>Preparing for con had already reawakened the hacker within, but being at con definitely energized and equipped me to push myself further. I want to make the most of the energy and expense that I invested, and finally tackle areas of interest that I postponed until now. I look forward to watching the talks that I missed live when they are posted.</p>
